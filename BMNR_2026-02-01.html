Below is an up-to-date, independent research brief on BitMine Immersion Technologies, Inc. (ticker: BMNR). All market and company data are as of January 30–February 1, 2026 unless noted.

1) Company Overview - What it does - BMNR has pivoted from bitcoin mining/hosting to an “ETH treasury” model: raising capital and acquiring ether (ETH) to hold on its balance sheet, plus staking and digital-asset advisory/leasing. In July–Nov 2025 the firm executed this pivot under new Chairman Thomas “Tom” Lee (Fundstrat). (sec.gov) - Where the money comes from (current mix is small vs. balance sheet) - Revenue streams today: staking income, equipment leasing, digital-asset consulting; legacy self-mining is being wound down. (sec.gov) - Scale of ETH holdings - Latest company-filed update (Jan 26, 2026): 4.243M ETH, 193 BTC, ~$682M cash, plus “moonshots” stakes ($200M Beast Industries; ~$19M Eightco/ORBS), for $12.8B total “crypto + cash + moonshots” at the then spot prices. BMNR says ETH stake equals ~3.52% of supply; targeted MAVAN staking rollout in Q1’26. (sec.gov) - Competitive positioning - Widely described as the world’s largest public ETH treasury (analog to MicroStrategy for BTC). A handful of large crypto-treasury peers exist, but few focused on ETH at this scale. (investopedia.com)
