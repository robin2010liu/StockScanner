1) Company Overview - What GH does: Guardant Health is a precision oncology diagnostics company. It develops blood‑ and tissue‑based tests across the cancer continuum: screening (Shield), treatment selection (Guardant360 liquid and tissue), minimal residual disease/recurrence monitoring (Reveal), and biopharma services/data. Revenue streams: Clinical oncology testing, Screening, and Biopharma & Data. (investors.guardanthealth.com) - Recent regulatory milestones: - Shield became the first FDA‑approved blood test for primary colorectal cancer (CRC) screening (July 2024), launched commercially in the U.S. with Medicare coverage (August 2024). CMS subsequently granted ADLT status (March 11, 2025), enabling market‑based Medicare pricing. (investors.guardanthealth.com) - Shield multi‑cancer detection (MCD) received FDA Breakthrough Device designation (June 3, 2025). (investors.guardanthealth.com) - Competitive position: A leader in liquid biopsy; first FDA‑cleared blood‑based CRC screen with Medicare coverage. Competes with Exact Sciences (stool‑based Cologuard and oncology assays), Natera (Signatera MRD), and Grail (MCED). Partnerships with Quest Diagnostics and PathGroup should broaden Shield’s reach.
