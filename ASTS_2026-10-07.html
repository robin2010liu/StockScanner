AST SpaceMobile (NASDAQ: ASTS) — Research Summary as of October 7, 2026

Current snapshot - Price: $60.65; Market cap: ~$18.1B; P/E: N/M (loss-making). Source: exchange quote at 23:40 UTC, Oct 7, 2026.

1) Company overview - What it does: AST SpaceMobile is building a space-based cellular broadband network that connects directly to unmodified 4G/5G smartphones using large phased‑array satellites (“BlueBird”). Commercial model is wholesale: ASTS partners with mobile network operators (MNOs), who use their licensed spectrum (and in some markets AST’s MSS/other spectrum) to offer service to subscribers. Revenue is expected from service access/roaming, government programs, and related equipment (gateways). (stockwatch.com) - Network status: First five commercial satellites (BlueBird 1–5) launched Sept 12, 2024. Subsequent launches increased in‑orbit fleet; management reported 13 spacecraft in orbit and preparation for beta service in 2026. (ast-science.com) - Key partnerships and contracts: Definitive commercial agreement with AT&T; strategic investments/commitments from AT&T, Google, Vodafone, and Verizon; 10‑year commercial agreement with stc group (Saudi Arabia) with $175M prepayment; growing U.S. government awards. (about.att.com) - Competitive position: Direct-to-device with unmodified phones at 4G/5G rates (ASTS has demonstrated ~99 Mbps peak to a standard smartphone in testing), a differentiator versus current text‑only D2D services. (sec.gov)

3) Business quality and moat - Technology: Very large LEO phased arrays with proprietary RF/beamforming and a custom ASIC enabling up to ~10 GHz processing bandwidth per satellite on Block‑2; ASTS has demonstrated near‑100 Mbps to a standard smartphone and 4G VoLTE calls with partners. Strong IP (≈3,900 claims worldwide; ≈2,100 granted/allowed as of Jun 30, 2026). (stockwatch.com) - Vertical integration: In‑house assembly, integration and test (AIT) in Midland, TX; company cites ~95% vertical integration on BlueBird 1–5. (ast-science.com) - Spectrum strategy: Uses partner MNO spectrum under the FCC’s Supplemental Coverage from Space (SCS) framework where applicable and augments with its own/controlled MSS holdings; also a June 2025 settlement framework with Ligado to access lower mid‑band in U.S./Canada. Regulatory tailwind: FCC adopted SCS rules in March 2024. (ast-science.com) - Commercial positioning: 60+ MNOs (>3B subs) including AT&T (U.S.), Vodafone (Europe/Africa), Verizon (U.S. MOU), stc (10‑year, $175M prepay), and others; growing U.S. government programs. Concentration risk exists as a few Tier‑1 partners drive early revenue. (about.att.com)
