Below is an up-to-date, structured research brief on Nebius Group N.V. (NASDAQ: NBIS) as of February 14, 2026.

1) Company Overview - What it does: Nebius is an AI-native cloud and infrastructure provider that designs/operates GPU-dense data centers and a proprietary cloud platform for model training and inference. The group also owns/operates Avride (autonomous mobility) and TripleTen (edtech), and holds stakes in ClickHouse and Toloka. It is the post-divestment successor to Yandex N.V., renamed in Aug 2024. Trading on Nasdaq resumed Oct 21, 2024 under ticker NBIS. (sec.gov) - Business model and revenue: Primarily sells AI compute capacity and platform services (AI Cloud, recent Token Factory inference platform) on multi‑year commitments and usage. Recent marquee contracts include Microsoft (5‑year, $17.4B base, up to ~$19.4B) and Meta ($3B, 5‑year). (investopedia.com) - Competitive set/positioning: Competes with “neocloud” specialists (CoreWeave, Crusoe, Lambda) and hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle). Nebius argues a full‑stack approach and AI‑specific software give it an efficiency edge. (sec.gov)
