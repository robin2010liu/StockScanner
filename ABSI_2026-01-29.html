Internal programs (e.g., ABS‑101 TL1A for IBD; ABS‑201 anti‑PRLR for androgenetic alopecia and, more recently, endometriosis) provide optionality for future milestone/out‑license economics or downstream product revenue. (globenewswire.com) - Positioning: Early-stage “TechBio” platform player in AI‑enabled biologics discovery.
