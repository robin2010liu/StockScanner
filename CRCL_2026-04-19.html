Before I dive in: there are two tickers “CRCL.” I assume you mean Circle Internet Group, Inc. (NYSE: CRCL), the USDC/EURC stablecoin issuer that listed on the NYSE on June 5, 2025. If instead you meant Corcel plc (AIM: CRCL) in London, say the word and I’ll pivot.

1) Company Overview - What it does: Circle runs a platform, network, and market infrastructure for fiat‑backed stablecoins and on‑chain payments/treasury. It issues USDC and EURC via regulated affiliates and earns primarily “reserve income” on the assets (T‑bills, overnight repos, cash) that back stablecoins in circulation. Other revenue comes from platform/services (APIs, accounts, payments/treasury, cross‑chain tech CCTP, tokenized money market fund USYC, and new products like Circle Payments Network and StableFX). (sec.gov) - Business model and revenue streams (FY25 mix): Reserve income ≈96% of revenue; “Other” ≈4% but growing from subscriptions/services and transactions. Distribution costs (notably to major partners like Coinbase) are netted in “RLDC” (revenue less distribution costs). (sec.gov) - Positioning: One of two global leaders in fiat‑backed stablecoins (with Tether as the much larger private rival). USDC year‑end 2025 circulation: $75.3B (+72% YoY). Circle aims to extend moat via compliance, banking/payments partnerships (e.g., Fiserv), an institutional payments network (CPN), and an L1 (Arc) tailored for stablecoin finance. (circle.com)
