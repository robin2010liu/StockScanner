Below is an up-to-date, investor-oriented brief on Archer Aviation (NYSE: ACHR) as of January 12, 2026. I include key primary sources and recent news throughout so you can dig deeper where it matters most.

1) Company Overview - What it does: Archer is developing electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft for short-haul urban/regional air mobility. Its first production aircraft is “Midnight,” a piloted, 4-passenger eVTOL optimized for rapid, back-to-back 10–20 minute flights. Revenue model will combine aircraft sales (to airlines/partners and defense) plus services/operations. (investors.archer.com) - How it makes/aims to make money: - Commercial: selling Midnight aircraft (indicative list price about $5 million), operating air taxi services with partners (e.g., United, Abu Dhabi Aviation), and software/ops support. (businesswire.com) - Defense/adjacencies: co-developing a hybrid VTOL platform with Anduril; supplying electric powertrain components for defense UAVs (e.g., Omen drone). (investors.archer.com) - Where it will launch: UAE first (Abu Dhabi “Launch Edition” program in 2026), U.S. thereafter; selected as Official Air Taxi Provider of the LA28 Olympics. (investors.archer.com) - Competitive position: One of a handful of late-stage eVTOL developers with meaningful capital, industrial partnerships (Stellantis manufacturing), and multiple FAA operational certificates (Part 145, 135, 141). Key competitor: Joby Aviation; others include EHang (China), Lilium, Vertical Aerospace. (investors.archer.com)
