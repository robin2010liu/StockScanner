Below is a compact, up-to-date equity research dossier on AST SpaceMobile (NASDAQ: ASTS) as of January 5, 2026.

1) Company overview - What it does: AST SpaceMobile (ASTS) is building a space-based cellular broadband network that connects directly to ordinary, unmodified smartphones via large low‑Earth‑orbit (LEO) satellites (“BlueBirds”). Service will be sold wholesale to mobile network operators (MNOs) and for government applications. (ast-science.com) - Core products and revenues - Space segment: BlueBird satellites (Block 1 in orbit; Block 2 next‑gen deploying) with ultra‑large phased arrays; BlueWalker‑3 was the pathfinder. Ground segment includes gateways and related equipment. Revenue to date has been mainly gateway deliveries, R&D/govt milestones; service revenue should ramp as coverage turns on. (ast-science.com) - Key commercial partners: Long‑dated commercial agreements with AT&T (through 2030), Verizon (U.S. coverage starting 2026), and Vodafone (through 2034), plus 50+ MNO partners globally. (about.att.com) - Current constellation status: First five commercial BlueBirds launched Sep 12, 2024; BlueBird‑6 (first Block‑2, ~2,400 sq ft array) launched Dec 23, 2025 on India’s LVM3. Additional launches planned through 2026 for continuous regional coverage. (about.att.com) - Competitive position: Early mover in broadband‑grade “direct‑to‑device” (D2D). Main competitors are SpaceX/Starlink’s Direct‑to‑Cell (with T‑Mobile and others), Lynk Global (SMS/data focus), and Apple/Globalstar (iPhone emergency/messaging). ASTS targets higher throughput (LTE/5G‑class) than most D2D peers. (reuters.com)
